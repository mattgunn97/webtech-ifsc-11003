FAQ's

  • Q: How many Rovers are there?

    A: There have been six successfully operated Mars rovers.

  • Q: Which missions are currently active?

    A: Curiosity and Perserverance are both currently active.

  • Q: Where there any failed missions?

    A: Mars 2 (PrOP-M Rover) failed its landing. Mars 3 (PrOP-M) landed successfully, but lost communication about 110 seconds after landing.

  • Q: Are there any planned missions?

    A: ESA's ExoMars rover Rosalind Franklin is confirmed ready to launch as of March 2022. ISRO has proposed a Mars rover as part of Mars Lander Mission.
